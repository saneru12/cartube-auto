Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common setup and compatibility questions.

How do I watch YouTube on Android Auto without rooting my phone?

Simply install the CarTube Auto APK, open the app, tap "Open Settings (1-Tap)", tap the Android Auto "Version" row 10 times to unlock Developer Mode, check "Unknown sources" inside Developer settings, and connect your phone to your car. Zero root or PC commands needed.

Why doesn't CarTube Auto show up on my car launcher yet?

Make sure you checked "Unknown sources" inside Android Auto's Developer settings (3-dots menu at top right) and also set "Application Mode" to "Developer". Then open "Customize launcher" in Android Auto settings on your phone, confirm CarTube Auto is checked, and reconnect USB/Wireless.

Does it work on Android 15, Android 16, Samsung One UI, and Xiaomi HyperOS?

Yes! CarTube Auto v2.9.5 natively supports Android 11 through Android 16. On Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO devices running MIUI or HyperOS, set CarTube Auto's Battery Saver to "No restrictions" and enable "Autostart" for uninterrupted background audio.

Can I use Google Maps or Waze while listening to YouTube?

Yes. CarTube Auto runs a dedicated automotive foreground media session so your music playlists and podcasts keep playing smoothly while Google Maps or Waze guides your route.
